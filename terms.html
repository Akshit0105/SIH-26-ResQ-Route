Terms & Conditions

ResQ-Route is an emergency coordination prototype. It does not replace emergency services, doctors, hospitals, ambulance operators, or other professional medical services.

1. General Terms

  • You must provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.
  • Access to emergency information is limited to the role and emergency-related purpose for which access is granted.
  • You must not misuse, copy, disclose, or attempt to access emergency information belonging to another person without authorization.
  • System availability, routing, ETA, location and other automated information may contain errors and must not be treated as the sole basis for medical decisions.
  • ResQ-Route may record system activity for security, auditing, troubleshooting and emergency coordination.

3. Data Protection & Controlled Access

  • Only information required for emergency coordination should be accessed or shared.
  • Medical information should be visible only to authorized participants involved in the active emergency.
  • Location data should be used for emergency response, tracking and coordination purposes.
  • Emergency contacts receive information only within the scope authorized by the patient/citizen and the system.
  • Users must report suspected unauthorized access, incorrect emergency information or security issues.

4. Emergency & Medical Disclaimer

ResQ-Route helps coordinate emergency information and communication. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, guarantee an ambulance arrival time, guarantee hospital admission, or replace professional medical judgment.

In a real emergency, users should also contact the appropriate local emergency service or medical professional.

5. Consent & Withdrawal

Consent should be collected before optional personal or medical information is used for the purposes described on this page. Where technically supported, consent may be withdrawn for future sharing, subject to records that must be retained for security, legal, audit or emergency-response purposes.

The permissions shown below are specific to the selected ResQ-Route role. They are not permission to access unrelated users, emergencies or hospital records.

6. Prototype Status

This version is designed for demonstration and SIH-style prototyping. Production deployment should add appropriate legal review, privacy notices, security controls, identity verification, audit controls, retention policies and integrations with authorized emergency/identity providers.
