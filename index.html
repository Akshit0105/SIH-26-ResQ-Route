RESQ-ROUTE

Frequently asked questions.

A few useful answers before humanity presses the giant red emergency button.

Do I need an account to request emergency assistance?

The emergency request flow is designed to allow emergency assistance without requiring the citizen to first create an account.

Who can access emergency information?

Information is intended to be shared according to the user's role and the emergency workflow, with access controlled by the backend security rules.

How does the ambulance dashboard work?

The ambulance dashboard receives assigned emergency information, route details, patient information and destination updates.

What does the hospital receive?

The hospital dashboard can receive incoming emergency details, patient information, ambulance ETA, vitals and preparation information.
